# Program Goals

## Mission

To provide undergraduate engineering students who have potential — but may not have equal access to opportunities — with the skills that will help them gain entrance to the tech industry and beyond.

## Why This Matters

Many talented students enter college with the drive and aptitude to succeed in a tech career, but face barriers that their peers with more access do not:

- Fewer industry connections and mentors
- Less exposure to internship recruiting processes
- Limited practice with technical interviews
- Uncertainty about how to present themselves professionally

UCP directly addresses these gaps through structured mentorship, practice, and transparency.

## Alumni as a Force Multiplier

After each cohort, cultivate a strong alumni network and integrate them into as many volunteer positions as possible. Alumni are already familiar with the program and offer invaluable technical guidance, best practices, and relevant advice — significantly aiding current cohort members in overcoming challenges like imposter syndrome.
