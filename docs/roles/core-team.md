# Core Program Team

While each role is vital to program execution, they may not require full-time staff. Detailed role descriptions and time commitments are available in the linked resources. Percentage time is calculated based on a 35-hour week or 8-month cohort.

---

## Regional Program Lead

**Time commitment:** ~700 hours (~50% time) over 8-month cohort

The Regional Program Lead is the central coordinator of the entire program.

**Responsibilities:**

- Manages program logistics, timeline, stakeholders, and budget
- Liaises between team and stakeholders
- Oversees Core Team execution
- Manages program timeline and milestones
- Authors/oversees program communications
- Confirms and creates workshop topics/agendas
- Identifies workshop speakers
- Reviews/implements workshop feedback

---

## Recruiting Team

**Time commitment:** ~436 hours (~30% time) over 8-month cohort

**Responsibilities:**

- Confirms/finalizes regional internship headcount with the Recruiting Team and organizations
- Creates marketing materials and leads application marketing/tracking
- Secures technical/behavioral interviewers for all interviews
- Schedules/reschedules all interviews
- Sources applicants via university partnerships and target schools
- Point of contact for recruiting questions on program Slack channels
- Follows up with fellows during interviews and extends offers
- Tracks competitor offers for metrics and industry insights
- Tracks company offers post-cohort and updates cohort graduate (alumni) tracker

---

## Workshop MC

**Time commitment:** ~40 hours (~1% time) during 8-month cohort

The Workshop MC is the main company representative at each workshop. This role can be combined with the Regional Program Lead if additional headcount is not available. Program alumni make ideal MCs.

**Responsibilities:**

- Main company representative at each workshop
- Manages speaker slides
- Creates workshop playlists
- Keeps sessions on time and fellows engaged
- Triages workshop questions

---

## Technical Lead

**Time commitment:** ~48 hours (~3% time) during 8-month cohort

**Responsibilities:**

- Manages technical portion of cohort acceptance interview process:
    - Questions bank
    - Volunteer interview training
    - Score cards/evaluation
    - Reviews scores, makes recommendations/decisions
- Manages mock interview question bank and score cards
- Manages internship interview question bank and score cards
- Authors/hosts technical training for volunteers
- Authors/hosts 75-min technical sessions for fellows during each workshop
- Hosts 60-min office hours between workshops
- Answers technical questions across all channels
- Creates/maintains code sharing platform for assignments and access

---

## Mentor & Volunteer Lead

**Time commitment:** ~116 hours (~8% time) over 8-month cohort

**Responsibilities:**

- Sources/assigns technical mentors for the cohort
- Liaises between Core Team, mentors, and fellows
- Manages mock interview sourcing/scheduling for each workshop
- Tracks mock interview/assignment submissions and follow-ups
- Provides mentors with workshop recaps
- Co-hosts office hours with Technical Lead
- Point of contact for mentor/volunteer questions on Slack
