# Roles & Responsibilities

Successful execution of a Career Prep program depends on a well-defined team structure. This section outlines every role — both on the Core Program Team and in the broader volunteer pool.

## Core Program Team

While each role is vital to program execution, they may not require full-time staff. Percentage time is calculated based on a 35-hour week or 8-month cohort.

| Role | Time Commitment | Key Focus |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| [Regional Program Lead](core-team.md#regional-program-lead) | ~700 hrs (~50% over 8 months) | Program logistics, stakeholders, budget |
| [Recruiting Team](core-team.md#recruiting-team) | ~436 hrs (~30% over 8 months) | Marketing, interviews, offers |
| [Workshop MC](core-team.md#workshop-mc) | ~40 hrs (~1% over 8 months) | Workshop facilitation |
| [Technical Lead](core-team.md#technical-lead) | ~48 hrs (~3% over 8 months) | Technical curriculum, interviews |
| [Mentor & Volunteer Lead](core-team.md#mentor-volunteer-lead) | ~116 hrs (~8% over 8 months) | Mentor sourcing, coordination |

## Volunteer Roles

| Role | Time per Engagement | Duration |
|------|-------------------|----------|
| [Mentors](volunteers.md#mentors) | ~2 hrs/week | Full cohort (8 months) |
| [Session Leads](volunteers.md#session-leads) | Up to 60 min/session | Per workshop |
| [Mock Interviewers](volunteers.md#mock-interviewers) | 70 min/interview | Per workshop |
| [Technical Interviewers](volunteers.md#technical-interviewers) | 70 min/interview | Entrance & internship interviews |
| [Behavioral Interviewers](volunteers.md#behavioral-interviewers) | 35 min/interview | Entrance & internship interviews |
| [Application Reviewers](volunteers.md#cohort-application-reviewers) | ~4 min/application | Application period |
