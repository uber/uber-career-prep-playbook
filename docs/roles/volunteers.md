# Volunteer Roles

Volunteers are essential for program execution, fulfilling various roles. The number of volunteers needed depends on their backgrounds, availability, and capacity.

---

## Mentors

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time commitment** | ~2 hours/week |
| **Duration** | Entire cohort (8 months) |
| **Eligibility** | Technical background required (must know how to code) |

**Responsibilities:**

- Conducts at least two 30-min meetings with their fellow between workshops
- Attends mentor onboarding and reviews training materials before cohort launch
- Grades fellow's technical practice assignments
- Reviews assignments and mock interview feedback with their fellow
- Updates mentee/mentor data in the mentor tracker for accountability
- Submits mid-year and year-end program feedback surveys

---

## Session Leads

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time commitment** | Up to 60 min/session |
| **Duration** | Per workshop |
| **Eligibility** | Technical or non-technical background, depending on the workshop theme |

**Responsibilities:**

- Participates in a 30-minute prep sync to align workshop expectations
- Develops/updates slides and contributes them to the main workshop deck
- Confirms which speakers will handle screen sharing
- Conducts a dry-run if necessary

---

## Mock Interviewers

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time commitment** | 70 minutes/interview (60 min interview + 10 min for feedback submission) |
| **Duration** | Per workshop |
| **Eligibility** | Technical background required (must know how to code) |

**Responsibilities:**

- Reviews mock interviewer training materials and question bank
- Reviews the fellow's resume before the interview
- Conducts the mock interview using the company's preferred platform
- Provides live feedback in the last 10 minutes, if time allows
- Submits feedback immediately post-interview

---

## Technical Interviewers

Conduct interviews to assess candidate suitability for both cohort admission and potential internship placement.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time commitment** | 70 minutes/interview (60 min interview + 10 min for feedback form submission) |
| **Duration** | Cohort entrance interviews and/or internship interviews |
| **Eligibility** | Technical background required (must know how to code); must have completed required company interview trainings |

**Responsibilities:**

- Reviews interviewer training documents and questions bank
- Attends the program-specific interviewer training
- Reviews the fellow's resume before the interview
- Submits feedback immediately post-interview

---

## Behavioral Interviewers

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time commitment** | 35 minutes/interview (30 min interview + 5 min for feedback form submission) |
| **Duration** | Cohort entrance interviews and/or internship interviews |
| **Eligibility** | No specific background requirements; must have completed required company interview trainings |

**Responsibilities:**

- Reviews interviewer training documents and questions bank
- Attends the program-specific interviewer training
- Reviews the fellow's resume before the interview
- Submits feedback immediately post-interview

---

## Cohort Application Reviewers

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time commitment** | ~4 minutes/application (total time determined by application-to-volunteer ratio) |
| **Duration** | Application review period |
| **Eligibility** | Technical background recommended |

**Responsibilities:**

- Attends application reviewer training
- Adds applicant summaries and reviewer accept/reject recommendations to the applicant tracker
