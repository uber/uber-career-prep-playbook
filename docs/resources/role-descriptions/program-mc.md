# Role Description: Program MC

## UCP Overview

Uber Career Prep (UCP) is an 8-month unpaid fellowship for undergraduate engineering students that brings transparency and raises visibility around the necessary steps to embark on a STEM career in tech.

We believe that everyone has potential, but not everyone has access to opportunity. This fellowship seeks to remove barriers and supports students’ right to learn and succeed.

There are 5 workshops hosted by Uber staff, which will cover both social and technical skills such as:

- Engineering competencies
- Communications
- Negotiations

Fellows will have four mandatory technical practice assignments, designed to enhance their interview readiness and based on the workshop curriculum.

In addition to other program components, Fellows will gain a software engineering mentor for the cohort's duration and take part in four mock interviews. They will then have an internship interview the year after the program ends.

## Role Summary

A program MC is nominated and selected based on region, technical skills, approachability, and charisma. They are the regional face of the cohort that they lead and MC 5 total workshops. They answer any questions asked during workshops, host all Q&A sessions, and moderate others as needed. Their personal and career experiences are inspiring; they embody Uber’s values and show outstanding work ethic. Their role includes but is not limited to:

- Being the main company representative at each workshop.
- Managing speaker slides.
- Creating workshop playlists.
- Keeping sessions on time and fellows engaged.
- Triaging workshop questions.

## Qualifications

- Passion for the mission and goal of Uber Career Prep
- Understanding of how the program works end-to-end
- Public speaking skills
- Good communication
- Good stakeholder engagement/ management skills
- Technical background (preferred)

## Main Responsibilities 

- Workshops
    - Create and present background/experience slide at Workshop 1.
    - MC and run all workshops.
    - Share screen during workshops and breaks.
    - Create and play cohort playlist during breaks.
    - Answer questions from Fellows during workshops.
- Ongoing
    - Participate in workshop agenda creation and maintenance:
        - Contribute session ideas.
        - Help identify and secure speakers.
- Offers/ Applications
    - Understand cohort selection process to answer Fellow questions.
    - Provide non-voting input in debrief sessions during final interviews.
