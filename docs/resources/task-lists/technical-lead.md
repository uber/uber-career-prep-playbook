# Task List: Technical Lead

## Timeline/ Task List 

Ongoing Task

- Asynchronously answering technical questions from mentors , fellows, and program team; this occurs as-needed. \[up to 15 min/ week for 35 weeks= 8.75 hours\]

### Before Cohort Interviews

#### Six Weeks Before Cohort Interviews Begin

- Confirm understanding of all technical materials with Emma Ziegellaub Eichler \[5 hours\]
    - Technical interview questions for cohort acceptances
    - Technical practice assignments
    - Technical presentations
    - Technical mock interview questions

#### Two Weeks Before Cohort Interviews Begin

- Schedule cohort selection interviewer training with Talent Acquisition Lead
- Edit and review presentations for mock interviewer/cohort interviewer/internship interviewer training training \[1 hour\]

#### One Week Before Cohort Interviews Begin

- Host and record cohort selection interviewer training with Talent Acquisition Lead \[ 30 min\]

### Cohort Interviews & Acceptances

#### During Cohort Interviews: ~2 Weeks

- Answer questions and consult with Program Team on technical cohort selection interviews \[~2 hours\]

#### After Cohort Acceptances

- Share Technical Practice 0 and due date with accepted fellows
- Schedule Technical Practice 0 office hours for fellows

#### After Mentor Selection

- Share Technical Practice 0 solution guide and grading deadline with mentors
- Attend mentor onboarding to answer any technical questions by mentors \[ 1 hour\]

### Before Workshop 1

#### Three Weeks Before Workshop 1

- Review & practice Technical Session 1 \[depending on level of comfort with materials\]
- Hold Technical Practice 0 office hours for fellows \[1 hour\]
- Optional: Hold mentor offices hours \[1 hour\]

#### Two Weeks Before Workshop 1

- Dry run for Technical Session 1 for program team & incorporate feedback \[75 min\]
- Host mock interviewer training + record session \[30min\]

#### Week Before Workshop 1

- RehearseTechnical Session 1 and review upcoming mock interview questions, technical practice assignment & solution guide, and be prepared to answer questions

#### Week of Workshop 1

- Schedule Technical Practice 1 office hours
    - Should be scheduled at the mid- point between Workshop 1 and 2
- Optional: Schedule mentor office hours and share date/ calendar invite with mentors
