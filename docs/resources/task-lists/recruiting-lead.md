# Task List: Recruiting Lead

**Authors:** Julia Alberts, Anne Marie Freeman  

## Before Application Launches

### Due Week of XXX

- Regional Scout Create applicant tracker \[ [Standard Template](../../resources.md#application-and-selection-trackers)\]

- Regional Scout Create Landing Page and Application

  - Update all wording and links to be Region specific (interview timeline, eligibility criteria, etc.)

  - Share both links for page and application with PgM Team for review before launch/accepting applications

  - Set up automated email confirmations for the application form

### Due Week of XXX

- Regional Scout Market application to the specific schools identified by Recruiting Team

- Regional Scout Work with current program partners as needed

- Regional Scout Create a list of scholarships, clubs, organizations, etc. that will help the PgM Team and volunteers review applications

- Regional PgM Help recruit volunteers for technical interviews as needed

- Regional PgMHelp recruit volunteers for behavioral interviews as needed

## Application Launches (DATE)

### Due DATE

- Regional Scout Opens application

- Regional Scout Reminds schools and partnerships about application opening

### Due DATE (cadence is set according to application review timeline) 

- Regional Scout Pull all applications into applicant tracker and share with Global PgM Team

## After Application Deadline (DATE)

### Due Week of DATE

- Regional Scout Meet with the PgM Team to review status of application reviews, interviewing needs, and expectations

### Due Week of DATE

- Regional TC Start scheduling candidates for interviews

- Regional Scout Guides fellows through local interview process

## Cohort Interviews (DATE)

### Due Week of DATE

- Regional Scout Answers any questions from candidates about the process

- Regional TC Reschedules interviews as needed

- Regional Scout Shares feedback from interviews with Global PgM Team

Cohort Entrance Acceptances (DATE)

### Due Week of DATE

- Regional Scout Secure headcount with Recruiting Team and/or Regional TA Manager to confirm the size of the cohort you should move forward with

- Regional PgM Update fellows on acceptance outcomes with action items

- Regional Scout Send rejections

## Workshops

### Due Week of DATE

- Regional Scout leads or finds a speaker as needed for Workshop 1-5 from the Recruiting Team

## Internship Interviews (DATE)

### Due Week of DATE

- Regional PgM Confirm technical interview volunteers for year-end internship interviews

### Due Week of DATE

- Regional TC Schedules internship interviews

### Due Week of DATE

- Regional Scout Internship interviews hosted

  - Track feedback and share with Global PgM Team

### Due Week of DATE

- Regional Scout Internship interview feedback collected

  - Advocate for candidates during the debriefs and escalate any urgent concerns to Regional TA Manager, following all company rules and regulations

  - Track feedback and share with Global PgM Team

### Due Week of DATE

- Regional Scout Secure final intern headcount with Recruiting Team

- Regional Scout Update Global PgM Team on outcomes of internship interviews

- Regional Scout Update fellows on outcomes of internship interviews

  - Regional Scout Send internship offers

  - Regional Scout Send rejections
