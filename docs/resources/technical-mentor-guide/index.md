# Technical Mentor Guide

A guide for technical mentors that explains how to support fellows through the technical homework and mock interviews.

It covers:

- **Homework:** what fellows are expected to do, how to grade each assignment, and how to give overall feedback.
- **Mock interviews:** what fellows should expect, and how mentors can review each mock with their fellow.

Replace placeholders such as `[your-program-email]` and the links to your feedback forms with your program's own, and refer to your company's junior-engineer interview bar when scoring.
