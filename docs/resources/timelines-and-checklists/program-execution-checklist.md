# Program Execution Checklist

### Initial (One–Time) Responsibilities

#### Cohort Kickoff

> Regional PgM Lead:

- Confirm program timeline
- Confirm workshop elements
- Confirm program team
- Create/ update internal site
- Create/ update decks
- Review applications
- Choose applicants to interview
- Review interview feedback
- Confirm cohort
- SWAG order
- Help Mentor/Volunteer Lead secure speakers for workshops (for duration of cohort)
- Help Mentor/Volunteer Lead find mock interviewers as needed (for duration of cohort)
- Help Mentor/Volunteer Lead schedule/reschedule mock interviews as needed (for duration of cohort)
- Create Zoom links for workshop sessions
- Add all workshops to calendar

> Alumni Lead/MC Lead:

- Confirm availability for all workshop dates
- Help find speakers/ mentors/ mock interviewers
- Familiarize yourself with programming across all regions
- MC for all workshops

> Technical Lead:

- Confirm understanding of all technical presentations
- Confirm understanding of all technical technical practice assignments assignments
- Confirm understanding of all technical mock interview questions
- Confirm ability to attend all sessions
- Confirm ability to host office hours
- Confirm ability to answer questions as they arise from fellows/ mentors

> TA Lead:

- Regional headcount estimate from leaders
- Create application landing page via company’s preferred platform
- Create application form
- Create application tracker
- Secure interviewers for initial cohort interviews
- Schedule interviews
- Share weekly top of funnel insights with PgM Team
- Work with PgM Team to confirm final cohort

> Mentor/Volunteer Lead:

- Secure volunteers for workshop 1
- Secure mentors for the entire cohort
- Set mentor/ fellow matches
    - Add all due dates to mentor calendars for visibility
    - Schedule/reschedule mock interviews (for duration of cohort)
    - Track fellow technical practice assignments submissions (for duration of cohort)
    - Track mentor grading for technical practice assignments (for duration of cohort)
    - Track mentor grading for mock interviews (for duration of cohort)

#### Once Cohort is Confirmed 

> Regional PgM Lead:

- Send welcome email to cohort and CC mentors
    - [Include Welcome Deck](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/16WbrE3-ttmswPstB2r7wDiM6-gUv3WG2Wl14EV_QOZ8/edit#slide=id.g2682e372091_0_0) in fellow email as a way to confirm application
- Email volunteer channels with an update on total number for the cohort and the bios of Fellows once cohort is confirmed
- Order SWAG for the year
- Manage budget

> Mentor/Volunteer Lead:

- Confirm mentors
    - Create mentor cohort email alias
    - Create mentor slack channel and pin relevant docs/ templates to the top
    - Create mentor main tracker and templates
- Send Fellow + Mentor introductions
- Host mentor onboarding (only before workshop 1)

> Technical Lead:

- Add office hours on the calendar
- Add technical practice assignments assignment due dates on the calendar
- Add technical practice assignments grading deadlines on the calendar
- Prepare to answer any technical questions from technical practice assignment 0

### Recurring Responsibilities (Before and After Each Workshop)

#### 4 Weeks Before Workshop 

> Regional PgM Lead:

- Create workshop deck
- Confirm agenda
- Share with Mentor/Volunteer Lead what volunteers are needed

> Mentor/Volunteer Lead:

- Call for volunteers for mock interviewers

> Technical Lead:

- Confirm office hours date
- Confirm technical practice assignments assignment due date
- Confirm technical practice assignments assignment grading deadline
- Host mock interviewer training
- Attend mentor onboarding (only before workshop 1)

#### 3 Weeks Before Workshop 

> Regional PgM Lead:

- Confirm agenda and share with Mentor/Volunteer Lead what volunteers are needed
- Confirm speakers
- Host dry runs as needed
- Update deck

> Mentor/Volunteer Lead:

- Send reminders to fellows to do their technical practice assignments
- Send mentor reminders to grade technical practice assignments as soon as it’s submitted

#### 2 Weeks Before Workshop 

> Regional PgM Lead:

- Finalize agenda and speakers
- Host dry runs as needed
- Update deck

> Technical Lead:

- Practice technical session presentation

#### 1 Week Before Workshop 

> Mentor/Volunteer Lead:

- Schedule all mock interviews- make sure all calendar invites are PUBLIC and do NOT require authentication to join Zoom
    - Include in all cal invites:
        - Zoom
        - CodeSignal
        - Fellow Name
        - Interviewer Name
        - Resume
        - Zoom Troubleshooting Doc
        - Name of POC in case there are issues
        - Mock Interview Questions Bank

> Technical Lead:

- Review technical session
- Review upcoming technical practice assignments assignment & solution guide

#### 3 Days Before Workshop 

> Regional Lead:

- Send reminder email to fellows with agenda and deck

#### 1 Day Before Workshop 

> Regional Lead:

- Check-in with all speakers
- Close editing to workshop deck

> Mentor/Volunteer Lead:

- Send reminder email to mock interviewers

#### Day of Workshop

> Regional PgM Lead:

- Take lots of photos!
    - Photos of all the students
    - Photos of the Uber presenters
- Send reminders to all speakers 30 minutes before session
- Manage zoom breakout sessions
- Have fellows submit feedback in closing session of workshop

> Alumni Lead/MC:

- Be ready to share screen
- Be ready with music to play during all breaks

> Mentor/Volunteer Lead:

- Be available to help troubleshoot if anything happens with mock interviews

> Technical Lead:

- Host one hour technical session

#### 2 Days After Workshop

> Regional PgM Lead:

- Send thank you + recap email to students for participating
    - Include technical practice assignments assignment
    - Include master deck + agenda + any other shared resources
    - Include feedback survey
- Update budget tracker for any spend associated with workshop

> Mentor/Volunteer Lead:

- Send followup emails to volunteers if they haven’t submitted their mock interview feedback yet

#### 2 Weeks After Workshop 

> Mentor/Volunteer Lead:

- Make sure all mock interview feedback is submitted by this time
- Track technical practice assignments submission from fellows
- Remind mentors to grade technical practice assignments

> Technical Lead:

- Host 1-hour optional technical office hours session to answer questions about the technical practice assignments

### End of Cohort 

> Regional PgM:

- Send recognition emails for volunteers
- Stay connected with TA Lead for conversion metrics
- Send cohort metrics to volunteers and key stakeholders
- Send congratulations email to cohort

> Mentor/Volunteer Lead:

- Send Email thank you’s for mentors and include direct managers
- Send feedback surveys to mentors
- Review mentor feedback

> TA Lead:

- Source volunteers for conversion interviews
- Schedule conversion interviews
- Track conversions
- Share conversions with Regional PgM
- Continually update alumni tracker at the beginning of each new internship / hiring cycle
