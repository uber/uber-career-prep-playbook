# Phase 3 — Post-Workshop: Internship Interviews & Outcomes

This phase covers the final interview stage, offer process, and program wrap-up.

**Key Requirements:**

- [x] Internship interviews should be conducted in a manner that mimics actual interviews.
- [x] Offer process should be managed efficiently and professionally.
- [x] Follow-up and relocation planning should be addressed.

---

## Internship Interviews and Outcomes

???+ abstract "Action"
    The Core Program Team and the Recruiting Team will collaborate to guide students through the final interview stage.

???+ info "Guidance"
    - Ensure a streamlined process to advance students efficiently through interview stages that aligns with your company's recruiting process.
    - Communicate a clear timeline to students regarding offer or rejection decisions.
    - Collaborate with the Recruiting Team to determine if program engagement and metrics will factor into offer evaluations.
    - Promptly monitor and record program outcomes, including:
        - Updating program trackers with final results
        - Informing relevant stakeholders
        - Acknowledging and thanking volunteers
    - Provide concluding remarks with encouragement for all cohort members, regardless of offer status.

???+ question "Considerations"
    - How will the metrics be communicated to key stakeholders?
    - What effect will this cohort's metrics have on subsequent cohorts?
    - What key takeaways were identified from the experience?
