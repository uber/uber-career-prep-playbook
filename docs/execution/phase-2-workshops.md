# Phase 2 — Workshops: Core Program Learning Sessions

This phase covers the preparation and execution of all workshop sessions across the cohort.

**Key Requirements:**

- [x] Curriculum delivery should be engaging and well-paced.
- [x] Balance lecture-style sessions with interactive activities and breakout groups.
- [x] Workshops should not conflict with students' exam schedules.

---

## Workshop Preparation

???+ abstract "Action"
    Prepare to host workshops using the Workshop Execution Checklist.

???+ info "Guidance"
    - Ensure the agenda is created with ~4 weeks lead-time to recruit and train volunteers as needed.
    - Schedule mock interviews at least one week in advance to confirm interviewer availability and set time aside for rescheduling.
    - Host a dry run with the MC to address all workshop one-off requirements.
    - Schedule dry runs with speakers as needed.

---

## Workshop Execution

???+ abstract "Action"
    Host workshops according to the program timeline.

???+ info "Guidance"
    - Conduct weekly Core Program Team meetings to discuss issues and questions.
    - Maintain program flexibility; promptly incorporate feedback for participant benefit.
    - Schedule breaks.
    - Design engaging agendas with diverse formats (e.g., panel discussions vs. single speakers).
    - Workshops will **not be recorded** to promote live participation.

???+ question "Considerations"
    - What is the degree of participant involvement in the workshops?
    - Does participant attention stay consistent, or are there drop-offs in engagement at certain times?
    - At what points are participants most likely to turn their cameras on or off?
    - How will your team address live engagement vs. AI bots and note-taking?
