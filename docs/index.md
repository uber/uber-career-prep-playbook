## About Uber Career Prep

Uber Career Prep (UCP) is an unpaid, virtual fellowship program for undergraduate engineering students. It aims to provide transparency and guidance for starting a STEM career in tech, focusing on removing barriers and supporting students' right to learn and succeed.

**At a glance:**

- 5 virtual workshops led by Uber staff, scheduled every 7 weeks over ~8 months
- 40 hours of instruction total
- 1:1 mentorship with an Uber software engineer
- 4 mock interviews and 5 technical practice assignments
- Invitation to interview for a paid internship at the end of the cohort

> A key driver of this program's success is that it's run by the Engineering Team — for future engineers.
