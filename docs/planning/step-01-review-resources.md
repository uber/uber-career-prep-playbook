# Step 1 — Review Program Resources and Create Program Templates

<span class="step-badge">Step 1 of 17</span>

???+ abstract "Action"
    - To align the program with company goals such as brand recognition, review all program documentation to gain a comprehensive understanding of the program.
    - Copy the program's templates to help guide you in the creation of your own program resources.

???+ info "Guidance"
    - Review of these documents helps clarify how the program operates, its intended outcomes, and how it contributes to broader company objectives.
    - Review of documents should be done **prior to moving to Step 2**.
    - Engage with whichever team runs the company's intern program.

???+ question "Considerations"
    - Will you utilize existing resources or develop new ones?
    - Since this program is heavily dependent on employee volunteers, does your company provide its employees with the freedom to take on initiatives outside of their daily work responsibilities?
