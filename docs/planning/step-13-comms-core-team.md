# Step 13 — Create a Communications Plan for Core Team

<span class="step-badge">Step 13 of 17</span>

???+ abstract "Action"
    Establish a communications plan to:

    1. Align stakeholders and Core Team on key milestones and deadlines
    2. Understand timing of communication to participants and volunteers

???+ info "Guidance"
    - Provide consistent status updates to stakeholders.
    - Coordinate with other program communication plans.
    - Consider internal communication norms and practices within your company.
    - Keep communications high-level with metrics; avoid logistical information unless asked for.
    - Allow time for follow-ups.
    - Keep key info in the email body — minimize the need to click on links.
    - Use catchy subject lines.
    - Add a "TL;DR" summary at the top.
    - Be direct while storytelling; avoid being overly transactional.

???+ question "Considerations"
    - **Who should send the communication?** The Regional Program Lead should send initial communications to leadership and key stakeholders to establish a clear point of contact.
    - **Personal or alias email?** Sending from a personal email creates accountability and awareness.
    - Who should be CC'd or BCC'd for advocacy and visibility?
    - What communication channel has been most effective for your team (email, Slack, etc.)?
