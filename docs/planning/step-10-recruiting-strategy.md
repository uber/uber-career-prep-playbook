# Step 10 — Align Recruiting Strategy & Confirm Eligibility Requirements

<span class="step-badge">Step 10 of 17</span>

???+ abstract "Action"
    Collaborate with the Recruiting Team and stakeholders to set the program's eligibility requirements based on your company's university recruiting strategy.

???+ info "Guidance"
    - Clearly define the purpose of this program and how it ties into university recruitment strategy.
    - Work with your Recruiting Team to enhance their current university recruiting strategy instead of reinventing the wheel.
    - Ensure eligibility criteria complies with regional laws and regulations for university hiring.
    - Employ "knockout" questions based on eligibility criteria to help filter applicants (e.g., graduation date, graduation month, required courses, experience level).
    - Provide clear and unambiguous definitions for eligibility criteria in the application.
    - Specify any disqualifying factors, such as AI usage or submission formatting errors.

???+ question "Considerations"

    **Demographics**

    - Is it legally permissible to collect this demographic data within the application? Note that in many countries, legally protected characteristics such as race and sex should not constitute eligibility criteria or influence participant selection.
    - Whether demographic information should be tracked and analyzed, and if so, when and how?
    - What kind of demographic information should be tracked (e.g., first-generation college student, whether qualified for financial aid, whether worked during the school year)?

    **Concentration/Major**

    - Which specific roles is the company recruiting for?
    - Are the names of majors or concentrations consistent across target universities?

    **Graduation Month/Year**

    - What is the required graduation date (exact month/year) for fellows to be eligible for internships post-program?
    - How many annual internship cycles does your company offer, and can students apply to all of them?
    - Are there any post-internship enrollment obligations for participating students?

    **Location**

    - What are the specific business requirements for the office(s) with open internship positions?
    - Which office location(s) currently have available internship headcount?
    - Is commuting to the office required for workshop attendance and/or internship participation?

    **General Eligibility**

    - Is concurrent enrollment during internships permitted?
    - Are relocation benefits available for interns who reside beyond commuting range?
    - Does your company hire international students with existing visas/permits, and/or provide visa/work permit sponsorship?
    - Are students from non-traditional educational backgrounds (e.g., non-4-year institutions or coding bootcamps) eligible?
    - What are your company's legal guidelines regarding student hiring?

    **Legal Compliance**

    - Ensure program compliance with laws and company policies covering data and hiring practices.
