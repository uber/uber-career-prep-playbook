# Step 8 — Confirm the Dates/Times of your Workshops

<span class="step-badge">Step 8 of 17</span>

???+ abstract "Action"
    Collaborate with your Core Program Team to schedule optimal dates and times for your workshops.

???+ info "Guidance"
    - Full-day workshops are **not recommended** — they require extended breaks (including lunch) and can lead to exhaustion for the Core Program Team and students.
    - Target 1st/2nd-year students for optimal internship and full-time opportunity pipelines; align academic years/selection with program duration and hiring needs.
    - For maximum impact, programs should be completed with enough time to teach the curriculum at a reasonable pace, as it only supplements current coursework.
    - The cohort must end before internships start, allowing time for interviews, offers, acceptances, and relocation.
    - The program's essential milestones and timeline are created by **planning backward from the internship start date**.
    - Having multiple internship cycles will improve placement chances based on internship opportunities and business needs.
    - Avoid scheduling workshops during exam weeks, as this impacts assignments and mock interview prep. Reference academic school calendars when finalizing dates.

???+ question "Considerations"
    - Should workshops be full-day or half-day?
    - Will the cohort span one or two academic years?
    - What are your company's internship start and end dates?
    - How many annual internship cycles are offered?
    - Do program dates align with recruiting deadlines?
    - Are there any company-specific scheduling constraints to consider?
    - How will company holidays/events affect volunteer participation?
    - Do regional holidays/cultural norms need consideration?
    - What are university exam periods and holiday schedules?
