# Step 11 — Create your Program Curriculum

<span class="step-badge">Step 11 of 17</span>

???+ abstract "Action"
    Develop the program curriculum to align with your company's success metrics.

???+ info "Guidance"

    **Workshop Structure**

    - Schedule interactive activities for post-lunch sessions to maintain engagement; prioritize technical and complex sessions for the morning.
    - Incorporate interactive activities, including breakout sessions, throughout the program to balance lectures and reduce the need for frequent breaks.
    - Incorporate both technical and professional skills training to create a well-rounded curriculum.
    - Organize workshops around specific themes to enhance session cohesion and minimize topic shifts.
    - Curriculum should complement — not replace — school content.
    - Curriculum should teach fellows how to apply learnings to interviews and internships.
    - Consult leadership on relevant industry tools, themes, and practices for internship prep session topics.
    - Incorporate workshop feedback and questions promptly.
    - Workshop sessions should be designed so lessons are applicable to future workshops, assignments, and practices.

    **Mock Interview Question Bank**

    - Questions should match the company's baseline for internship question difficulty.
    - Questions should align with technical practice assignment themes and build in difficulty over time.
    - There should be a handful of questions to choose from for each workshop in case fellows are able to solve more than one during the provided time.

    **Technical Practice Assignments**

    - Themes should align with mock interview questions for practice and familiarization before the interview.
    - Technical practice assignments build on each other, starting with easier themes as the foundation.

???+ question "Considerations"
    - Will the curriculum target single or multiple graduation years and still remain relevant to all?
    - How many total instruction hours are planned, and can they be evenly distributed across all workshops?
    - Will the curriculum include specific engagement events?
    - What key technical or behavioral competencies should fellows develop?
    - Is there sufficient technical preparation and practice for student success?
    - Will sessions be lecture-based, interactive, or include breakouts?
    - Does the program offer enough session variety to maintain engagement?
    - Does the curriculum address relevant industry trends and student challenges?
