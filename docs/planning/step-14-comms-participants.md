# Step 14 — Create a Communications Plan for Participants and Volunteers

<span class="step-badge">Step 14 of 17</span>

???+ abstract "Action"
    Establish a communications plan in alignment with workshop dates.

???+ info "Guidance"
    - Coordinate with other program communication plans to avoid overlapping messages and asks to the same audience.
    - Consider internal communication norms and practices within your company.
    - Clearly state "ask" due dates.
    - Allow time for follow-ups.
    - Keep key info in the email body — minimize the need to click on links.
    - Use catchy subject lines.
    - Add a "TL;DR" summary at the top.
    - Be direct while storytelling; avoid being overly transactional.
    - Include the benefits of participation in the program as a volunteer.

???+ question "Considerations"
    - **Who should send the communication?**
        - Leadership should send initial volunteer communications to demonstrate support for their time and effort.
        - The Core Program Team can send any follow-up emails as needed.
    - **Personal or alias email?** Sending from an alias enables team alignment and cross-functional support as needed.
    - **Where should responses be directed?** Responses should be directed to an alias to ensure team alignment and cross-functional support.
    - Who should be CC'd or BCC'd?
    - What communication channel has been most effective for your team (email, Slack, etc.)?
