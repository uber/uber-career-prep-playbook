# Step 7 — Confirm Size of your Cohort

<span class="step-badge">Step 7 of 17</span>

???+ abstract "Action"
    Determine the size of your cohort.

???+ info "Guidance"
    - Confirm the anticipated number of interns to be hired in the upcoming cycle with the Recruiting Team.
    - To effectively test the curriculum and maximize ROI, consider starting with a moderate-sized initial cohort. This allows for validation of the curriculum's success before scaling to a larger number of students.
    - **Begin with a cohort of at least 8–10 students** for these reasons:
        - To account for potential absences and maintain engagement in breakout sessions.
        - Smaller cohorts may create undue pressure on students and not fully utilize volunteer time.
        - This size allows for easy pairing of students for activities.
        - Larger cohorts could overwhelm the Core Program Team during initial implementation.

???+ question "Considerations"
    - What percentage of internship opportunities would this program require if all students pass the internship interviews?
    - Should the cohort number be adjusted up or down based on that percentage?
    - Are there similar programs for potential marketing or process collaboration?
    - Will there be adequate volunteer support for the entire cohort?
