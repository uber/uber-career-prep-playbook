# Step 6 — Confirm Length of the Program

<span class="step-badge">Step 6 of 17</span>

???+ abstract "Action"
    Space your workshops at least **7 weeks apart** to allow time for assignments, grading, interview feedback, and office hours.

???+ info "Guidance"
    - The program should end with sufficient time for interviews and offers before internships begin, so that fellows can participate in the internship selection process.
    - Confirm your company's requirements and qualifications for interns.
    - Holidays can disrupt the cohort schedule due to time off for students and volunteers.
    - Keep in mind annual planning dates and other key events in your company's calendar that may impact volunteer availability.

???+ question "Considerations"
    - What are the internship start dates?
    - How many yearly internship cycles are there?
    - Can graduates of the program (alumni) be placed in any available internship cycle?
    - Do cohort dates align with multiple internship cycles?
    - Will the cohort schedule overlap with holidays?
    - What is the estimated length of assignments, and what's the reasonable completion time?
    - How often should mentor–fellow meetings be held?
