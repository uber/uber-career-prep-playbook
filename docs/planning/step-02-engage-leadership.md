# Step 2 — Engage your Leadership Team

<span class="step-badge">Step 2 of 17</span>

???+ abstract "Action"
    Obtain Leadership Team approval for program support and advocacy.

???+ info "Guidance"
    - Leadership Team approval is helpful in acquiring internship headcount, budget, and volunteer resources.
    - Start stakeholder discussions early — they are time-consuming.
    - Schedule extra time to prepare tailored stakeholder proposals and presentations.
    - Include the Recruiting Team in the Leadership Team for internship opportunity headcount/placement alignment.
    - Prepare a proposal that includes:
        - A storyline of why this program is important
        - Any metrics that help support your proposal
        - The overall timeline of the program
        - Clear program needs such as:
            - The involvement expected from leadership
            - An estimated budget during your initial conversation with leadership *(refer to the [budget section](step-09-budget.md) for guidance)*
            - An estimated Program Team headcount

???+ question "Considerations"
    - Did you ensure time for leadership discussions before deadlines?
    - Did you customize presentations for each stakeholder for maximum buy-in?
    - Do you have support and advocacy during stakeholder meetings?
