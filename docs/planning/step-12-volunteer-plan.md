# Step 12 — Create Volunteer Plan

<span class="step-badge">Step 12 of 17</span>

???+ abstract "Action"
    Determine volunteer needs, target audience, and engagement strategies.

???+ info "Guidance"
    - Target at least **1:1 technical volunteer to cohort size** for:
        - Entrance, internship, and mock interviews
        - 1:1 mentorship
    - Target **1:1 behavioral volunteer to cohort size** for entrance interviews.
    - Recruit a larger volunteer pool to allow for replacements.
    - Engage early-career volunteers to build network and culture.
    - Avoid over-using the same volunteers.
    - Ensure all volunteer recruits are eligible.
    - Ensure all volunteers understand their roles and responsibilities.
    - Newer volunteers may not be ideal for complex mentorship.
    - Train interview volunteers to match company standards.
    - Show appreciation to volunteers based on your company's recognition platforms (e.g., all-hands shout-outs, company newsletters, SWAG, performance and promotion outcomes).

???+ question "Considerations"
    - Can volunteers hold multiple roles?
    - Are specific background requirements needed for roles?
    - What are the volunteers' time zones?
