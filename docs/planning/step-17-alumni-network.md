# Step 17 — Creating an Alumni Network

<span class="step-badge">Step 17 of 17</span>

???+ abstract "Action"
    Cultivate a strong alumni network.

???+ info "Guidance"
    Integrate alumni into as many aspects of your program as possible. They are familiar with the program and offer invaluable technical guidance, best practices, and relevant advice — significantly aiding current cohort members in overcoming challenges like imposter syndrome.

???+ question "Considerations"
    - Does your company allow external guest speakers if your alumni no longer work at your company?
