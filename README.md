# Uber Career Prep — Open Source Playbook

A strategy and execution playbook for building a Career Prep program that helps undergraduate engineering students enter the tech industry.

Uber Career Prep (UCP) is an unpaid, virtual fellowship program that provides transparency and guidance for starting a STEM career in tech. This playbook documents everything required to build a similar program at your organization — from securing leadership buy-in to running workshops, managing volunteers, and placing fellows into internships.

## 📖 Read the Playbook

**[uber.github.io/uber-career-prep-playbook](https://uber.github.io/uber-career-prep-playbook/)**

The full playbook is published as a searchable, navigable website covering:

- **17-step planning guide** — from reviewing resources to building your alumni network
- **Roles & responsibilities** — Core Program Team and volunteer role breakdowns with time commitments
- **Program execution framework** — three-phase guide through fellow selection, workshops, and internship outcomes
- **Open-source resources** — templates, checklists, trackers, and role descriptions

---

## About the Program

Fellows of the program attend 5 virtual workshops led by Uber staff, scheduled every 7 weeks over approximately 8 months, completing 40 hours of instruction. Each fellow receives 1:1 mentorship from an Uber software engineer, participates in 4 mock interviews, and completes 5 technical practice assignments. At the end of the cohort, fellows who meet program requirements are invited to interview for a paid internship.

Workshop topics include technical mock interviews, clear code and communication, negotiations, technical competencies and problem solving, and LinkedIn and resume best practices.

> A key driver of this program's success is that it's run by the Engineering Team — for future engineers.
