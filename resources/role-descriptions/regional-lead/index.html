    Role Description: Regional Lead¶

    UCP Overview¶

    Uber Career Prep (UCP) is an 8-month unpaid fellowship for undergraduate engineering students that brings transparency and raises visibility around the necessary steps to embark on a STEM career in tech.

    We believe that everyone has potential, but not everyone has access to opportunity. This fellowship seeks to remove barriers and supports students’ right to learn and succeed.

    There are 5 workshops hosted by Uber staff, which will cover both social and technical skills such as:

    • Engineering competencies
    • Communications
    • Negotiations

    Fellows will have four mandatory technical practice assignments, designed to enhance their interview readiness and based on the workshop curriculum.

    In addition to other program components, Fellows will gain a software engineering mentor for the cohort's duration and take part in four mock interviews. They will then have an internship interview the year after the program ends.

    Role Summary¶

    Regional Program Leads are chosen for their region, skills, accessibility, and capacity for managing multiple tasks concurrently. They lead their regional program, ensure its success, and supervise the Core Program team's* work. They are fully informed about program details and connect the Core Program team with stakeholders. They should be enthusiastic about inclusion and adaptable to change. They attend all workshops and additional volunteer training or office hours as required.

    This person’s responsibilities include but are not limited to:

    • Manages program logistics, timeline, stakeholders, and budget.
    • Liaises between team and stakeholders.
    • Oversees Core Team execution.
    • Manages program timeline and milestones.
    • Authors/oversees program communications.
    • Confirms and creates workshop topics/agendas.
    • Identifies workshop speakers.
    • Reviews/implements workshop feedback.

    A full list of tasks and responsibilities can be found here.

    Qualifications¶

    • Proven experience in the following areas:
      • Leadership
      • Organize and execute
      • Fearless and truthseeking
      • Communications
      • Stakeholder management
    • Public speaking skills

    Responsibilities and Time Commitment¶

    The Regional Program Lead will need to dedicate at least 50% time to this role as the majority of tasks owned are ongoing for the duration of the cohort.

    Category Tasks
    Stakeholder Management • Serve as the primary representative for presentations and meetings.
    • Possess a thorough understanding of the program's mission and goals.
    • Create partnership proposals, and authors the cohort retrospective.
    Budget • Benchmark program budgets for alignment.
    • Forecast and monitor spending.
    • Manage vendor relations and purchase orders.
    • Procure SWAG as necessary.
    Workshops • Source and coordinate speakers.
    • Collaborate with Volunteer Lead on needs.
    • Develop workshop presentation decks.
    • Conduct speaker dry runs as necessary.
    • Coordinate with/be ready to MC workshops.
    • Provide MC IT support (breakout rooms, polls, etc.).
    • Organize cohort engagement activities.
    • Send speaker reminders (week-of/day-of).
    • Prepare fellow communications and recaps.
    • Manage workshop feedback.
    Recruiting Alignment • Confirm following year's intern headcount with TA Lead.
    • Verify UCP cohort headcount and hiring locations.
    • Align on internship timelines, graduation years, application questions, and workshop dates.
    • Co-create marketing materials.
    Technical Alignment • Ensure up-to-date technical materials with Technical Lead.
    • Assist with documentation management as needed.
    • Update curriculum based on feedback.
    • Co-create onboarding/volunteer training.
    Ongoing • Monitor/triage Slack channels/team aliases.
    • Lead bi-weekly Core Program Team meetings.
    • Develop and maintain program documentation.
    • Facilitate regional alumni engagement.
    • Manage program metrics and KR deliverables.

    *Core Program Team Members
