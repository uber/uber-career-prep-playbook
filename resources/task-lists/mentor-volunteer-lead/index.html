    Task List: Mentor and Volunteer Lead¶

    This document outlines the key tasks for the UCP Regional Mentor and Volunteer Lead, organized by program phase and workshop. The goal is to provide a clear, actionable, and easy-to-follow guide for all responsibilities.

    Phase 1: Before Cohort Launches¶

    1. Mentor and Volunteer Recruitment¶

    • Develop Strategic Outreach Plan: Create a comprehensive plan for mentor recruitment, covering the entire cohort duration.
      • Determine outreach sequence (e.g., past volunteers, regional tech sites, Eng Slack channels).
      • Create mentor recruitment communications, including the global volunteer interest form.
      • Execute email communications based on the defined sequence.

    2. Mentor Onboarding & Training¶

    • Develop comprehensive onboarding and training materials for the entire program.
    • Host onboarding and training sessions (e.g., NAMER/LATAM 2025 Version).
    • Distribute post-training feedback forms.
    • Review and share mentor training feedback with the facilitator.

    3. Mentor Tracker Management¶

    • Establish a new Mentor Tracker spreadsheet (use consistent naming convention).
    • Remind mentors that provided information is for internal use only.

    4. Mentor & Volunteer Selection¶

    • Review highly-rated mentors from previous programs.
    • Address remaining volunteer needs using the Volunteer Outreach Plan.
    • Maintain a list of substitute mentors.

    5. Mentor-Fellow Matching¶

    • Select mentors for the upcoming cohort.
    • Match mentors with fellows based on region, language, and interests.
    • Finalize and communicate matches approximately two weeks before Workshop 1 and/or Technical Practice Assignment 0 deadline.

    6. Mentor Communication & Introductions¶

    • Create a dedicated Mentor Slack channel.
    • Share a responsibilities one-pager (or Slack Canvas) and pin it in the channel.
    • Email selected mentors confirmation of participation, next steps, and training schedule (e.g., EMEA 2024 Version).
    • Following mentor onboarding, send introductory emails to newly formed mentor-mentee pairs.

    7. Prepare for Workshop 1¶

    • Mentee-Mentor Expectations: Create a slide outlining overall communication guidelines (e.g., 1:1 meeting frequency, responsibilities).
    • Mock Interview Setup:
      • Select mock interviewers (preference for tenured company employees).
      • Ensure mock interviewers complete required training.
      • Send participation confirmation emails and calendar invites.
      • Schedule and send all mock interview invitations.

    Phase 2: Cohort Starts¶

    Workshop 1¶

    During Workshop 1¶

    • Present Fellows-Mentor expectations slides.
    • Reschedule mock interviews only if requested before the day of the interview.
    • Decide on rescheduling exceptions (e.g., one reschedule allowed per Fellow per cohort vs. one per mock interview).

    After Workshop 1¶

    Feedback & Debrief:¶

    • Ensure all mock interview feedback is submitted (crucial for mentor-mentee debriefs).
    • Remind mentors to review mock interview feedback with their mentees 1:1.

    Office Hours:¶

    • Schedule and host office hours to support Mentors.
    • Schedule and host office hours to support Fellows (suggestion: book on the same day, e.g., Fellows in morning, Mentors in afternoon, to share insights).

    Community Support:¶

    • Address community questions and incorporate them into the program's FAQ document.
    • Share written responses in relevant Slack channels for wider visibility.

    Mentor Slack Updates:¶

    • Send "Updates & Reminders" message to Mentors (e.g., "Your Mentees completed Workshop 1 & Mock Interview 1, remember to review mock interview feedback with your mentees").
    • Ensure Mentors have graded homework by the outlined deadline.
    • Ensure Mentors have reviewed homework with Fellows.
    • Send reminders of upcoming office hours.

    Before Workshop 2, 3, & 4 (Recurring Tasks)¶

    • Secure mock interviewers.
    • Schedule mock interviews.
    • Confirm general session leads/speakers as needed.
    • Before Workshop 3 Only: Create mid-program mentor feedback survey and be ready to send it.
    • Before Workshop 5 Only:
      • Confirm general session leads/speakers as needed.
      • Create year-end mentor survey to send to Mentors.
      • Create year-end mentor survey section to send to Fellows (to understand mentor ratings/engagement).

    During Workshop 2, 3, 4, & 5 (Recurring Task)¶

    • Reschedule mock interviews as needed.
    • During Workshop 5 Only: Send final end-of-program mentor feedback survey.

    After Workshop 2, 3, & 4 (Recurring Tasks)¶

    • Feedback Review: Review workshop feedback form responses to identify any necessary changes for future workshops.
    • Follow-up: Follow up with any inactive Mentors or Fellows (loop in the UCP PgM team as needed).
      • If a Mentor has not met with a Fellow, consider striking their indicator and reassigning the Mentor.
    • Mock Interview Feedback: Ensure all mock interview feedback is submitted.
    • Office Hours: Schedule and host office hours to support Mentors and Fellows.
    • Mentor Slack Updates:
      • Send "Updates & Reminders" message to Mentors (e.g., "Mentees completed Workshop [X] & Mock Interview [X], reminder to review mock interview feedback with Fellows").
      • Ensure Mentors have graded homework by the outlined deadline.
      • Ensure Mentors have reviewed homework with Fellows.
      • Send reminders of upcoming office hours.

    Phase 3: After Cohort Ends¶

    After Workshop 5¶

    • Year-End Review for UCP:
      • Review mentor feedback and collect quotes for inclusion in the year-end review.
    • Workshop 5 Feedback & Mentor Recognition:
      • Review Workshop 5 feedback forms.
      • Extract thank-you quotes for Mentors (for performance/promotion emails).
      • Share student thank-you quotes with Mentors.
